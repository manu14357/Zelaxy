# Enrich Block

The Enrich block integrates Enrich.so data enrichment into your workflows. Use it to turn an email into a full profile, find work emails from LinkedIn, verify deliverability, look up phone numbers, and search people or companies.

## Overview

| Property | Value |
|----------|-------|
| **Type** | `enrich` |
| **Category** | tools |
| **Color** | `#E5E5E6` |

## When to Use

- Resolve an email address into a full person or LinkedIn profile
- Find a person's work email from their LinkedIn URL
- Verify whether an email address is valid and deliverable
- Discover a phone number from a LinkedIn profile
- Look up company details for sales or research workflows
- Search for people or companies to power lead-generation pipelines

## Configuration

### Operation
Dropdown selecting which Enrich.so action to run. Options: Email to Profile, Email to Person (Lite), LinkedIn Profile, Find Email, LinkedIn to Work Email, Verify Email, Phone Finder, Company Lookup, Search People, and Search Company. Required.

### API Key
Your Enrich.so API key (stored as a password field). Required.

### Email
The email address to enrich or verify. Shown for the Email to Profile, Email to Person (Lite), and Verify Email operations.

### LinkedIn URL
The LinkedIn profile URL to enrich. Shown for the LinkedIn Profile, LinkedIn to Work Email, and Phone Finder operations.

## Inputs & Outputs

- **Inputs**: `operation` (string), `apiKey` (string), `email` (string), `linkedinUrl` (string)
- **Outputs**: `profile` (json), `email` (string), `person` (json), `company` (json)

## YAML Example

```yaml
enrich_1:
  type: enrich
  name: "Enrich"
  inputs:
    operation: "enrich_email_to_profile"
    apiKey: "{{ENRICH_API_KEY}}"
    email: "{{start.input}}"
  connections:
    outgoing:
      - target: next-block-id
```
